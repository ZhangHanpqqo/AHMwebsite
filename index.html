About A Happy Mennn (AHM)

A Happy Mennn is an interdisciplinary multimedia artist trio and research group formed by Anqi Liu, Han Zhang, and Mingyong Cheng. AHM's works address the lived experience of immigrant Asian women artists building career, practicing, and living in the West, via the use of technology, sound, and free improvisation performing arts.

Anqi Liu is a music composer, sound artist, interdisciplinary/multimedia artist, photographer, and filmmaker.

Han Zhang is a multimedia artist, computer musician, performer, and engineer.

Mingyong Cheng is a new/digital media artist, interdisciplinary visual artist, and creative technologist.

More on AHM's ongoing project: Peeling Cycle I (New York City premiere) and Peeling Cycle II (San Diego iteration).
